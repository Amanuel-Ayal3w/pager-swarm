# Voice

Tone: concise, impersonal, calm, conversational, concrete.

- Keep sentences around 10 words; that is the measured average across the site's copy.
- Write headings in sentence case; only 1 of 28 measured headings use Title Case.
- Avoid exclamation marks; the measured copy almost never uses them.
- Label buttons with short nouns or phrases, about 2 words long.
- Use concrete numbers where you can; the copy leans on them.

## Examples (new sentences in this voice)

- **headline**: Plan less. Ship more.
- **button**: Start now
- **empty state**: Nothing here yet.
- **error**: That didn't work. Try again.
